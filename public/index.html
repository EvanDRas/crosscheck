Welcome to Crosscheck — one free key and you're live

Crosscheck runs on your machine with your own free API keys: your lookups stay yours, and nobody can turn the service off. Try the DEMO ticker first if you like — it needs no keys at all.

  1. Create a free account at finnhub.io and copy the API key from the dashboard. Required — powers quotes, fundamentals, analysts, earnings.
  2. Optional but recommended: a free key from tiingo.com (Account → API) — powers price charts, 52-week history, and the Time Machine.
  3. Optional: an email for SEC EDGAR's fair-access policy (their servers like to know who's calling).
